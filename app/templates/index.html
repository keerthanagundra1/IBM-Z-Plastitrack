🔒 What we read and keep

  • Read: the order email text you provide.
  • Keep: only line items: store, date, product, quantity, price.
  • Removed first: names, email addresses, street addresses, phone numbers, order numbers, card digits.
  • Raw emails are discarded right after parsing and never written to disk.
  • All AI runs on this LinuxONE server: the ML model and the IBM Granite LLM. No third-party AI APIs, no CDNs.
